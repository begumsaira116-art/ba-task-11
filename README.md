Analyze the Bubble Chart

How to identify the most significant category

Look for a large bubble with positive profit.

A large bubble indicates high sales. If its color also represents strong positive profit, that sub-category may be particularly valuable to the business.

How to identify the least significant category

Look for small bubbles with low sales.

Then check the profit values.

A sub-category with low sales and negative profit may need further investigation. However, low sales alone do not necessarily mean that a product is unimportant.

Important

Don't assume the largest bubble is the most profitable. Bubble size represents Sales, not Profit
